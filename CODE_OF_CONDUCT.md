# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, safe, and professional environment for all contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on ideas, not individuals.
- Assume positive intent.
- Provide actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private data without permission.
- Abusive language, threats, or intimidation.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after warning.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to project maintainers via private channels listed in README.md.

When reporting, include:

- what happened
- where it happened
- who was involved
- evidence (if available)

## Enforcement

Maintainers may apply actions including:

- warning
- temporary restriction
- permanent removal from project spaces

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- repository discussions and pull requests
- issue tracker and review comments
- official communication channels

## Good Faith

All reports are reviewed in good faith, with confidentiality as far as possible.
