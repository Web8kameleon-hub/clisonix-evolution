# NRW Outreach Playbook (German)

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

- Mid-size logistics companies in NRW
- Facility and security operators with on-site teams
- Operations managers who need alert delivery even during internet outages

## One-Sentence Offer

Clisonix NodeSMS liefert kritische Nachrichten mit API plus LoRa-Fallback, damit Alarm- und Einsatzkommunikation auch bei Netzstoerungen weiterlaeuft.

## 20-Min Demo Agenda

- 5 min: Ihr aktueller Alert-Flow und Ausfallrisiken
- 8 min: Live-Demo NodeSMS + LoRa fallback path
- 4 min: Monitoring, Logs, SLA Optionen
- 3 min: Pilotumfang, Zeitplan, naechste Schritte

## Pilot Offer (14 Days)

- Scope: 1 use case, 1 alert channel, 1 ops team
- KPI baseline: delivery time, failure rate, operator escalation time
- Output: pilot report + production rollout recommendation

## Weekly Activity Target

- 30 new contacts
- 12 first emails sent
- 6 follow-up chains active
- 4 demo calls booked
- 1 pilot signed
